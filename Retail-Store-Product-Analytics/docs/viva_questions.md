# Viva / Presentation Questions

### 1. What is Product Analytics?
Product Analytics is the analysis of product-level sales, quantity, revenue, profit and related metrics to understand product performance.

### 2. What is the objective of your module?
To identify top-selling and profitable products, compare categories, study sales trends, and provide an interactive dashboard for decision-making.

### 3. Which tools did you use?
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Plotly and Streamlit.

### 4. What is revenue?
Revenue is the total amount generated from sales.

### 5. What is profit?
Profit is the amount left after subtracting total cost from sales.

### 6. Why did you create KPI cards?
They give a quick summary of the most important business metrics.

### 7. Why Top 10 products?
It makes the dashboard easier to read and focuses attention on the strongest products.

### 8. How are products classified?
Using median revenue and median profit. High/high products are Star Products, high revenue/low profit are Volume Products, low revenue/high profit are Potential Products, and low/low are Weak Products.

### 9. What is the purpose of the filters?
Users can focus the analysis on a selected category, year or store.

### 10. What is the dashboard's main benefit?
It converts raw transaction data into visual and actionable product-level insights.
